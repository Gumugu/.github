## 👋 About us

**PT Gumugu Teknologi Kreatif** is an Indonesian EdTech company based in Banda Aceh and Jakarta.

Many schools in Indonesia still run admissions, tuition and communication on paper, spreadsheets and WhatsApp. Enterprise systems are too expensive and complex for them, while generic tools don't fit local needs. We build affordable, modular software that helps institutions go digital, from rural Islamic boarding schools (*pesantren*) to private schools, universities and education associations.

> *Gumugu adalah perusahaan EdTech Indonesia yang membangun perangkat lunak terjangkau untuk membantu sekolah, pesantren, kampus dan asosiasi pendidikan beralih ke digital.*

## 🧩 Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gumugu Admission** | Online student admissions (PSB/PPDB) with parent portal and local payments (Midtrans, bank transfer) |
| **Gumugu School System** | School finance: tuition (SPP), student savings, budget requests, receipts, accountability reports and Excel/print exports |
| **Gumugu CMS** | Multi-site CMS for school and university websites, with a bilingual (ID/EN) block editor, revision history and e-learning add-ons |
| **Gumugu Events** | Event registration, membership, payments and bulk certificate generation for education associations and conferences |

- **Frontend:** Bootstrap, vanilla JavaScript, PWA

- **Documents:** server-side PDF generation for receipts, reports and certificates

## 🌱 What we care about

- **Affordable by design:** setup fee plus annual licence, priced for small schools
- **Built for local realities:** Indonesian workflows, Bahasa Indonesia first, bilingual where needed
- **Simple to use:** tools that teachers, admins and parents can use without training manuals
